How to build a simple wardrobe around one leather jacket

In short: You do not need ten jackets. One good leather jacket and a few basics will carry you through most of the year here.

Most people overbuy. They pick up a jacket every season, wear each one twice, and still feel like they have nothing to wear. A simpler approach works better, especially with our weather, where real jacket months run from about October to March.

Start with one jacket that goes with everything

Black or a deep brown, clean lines, no heavy hardware. That jacket will sit over a white tee in October and over a hoodie in January, and it will not look out of place at work or at dinner. Ours that people reach for most is the Structured Trench Coat for a smarter look, or a plain leather jacket if you want something easier.

Keep the layers underneath plain

Two or three solid tees, one good hoodie, a shirt you can wear open or buttoned. Plain colours do more work than prints, because they let the jacket be the piece people notice. A heavy cotton tee under a jacket sits better than a thin one, and does not cling once the day warms up.

Trousers that are not jeans

Jeans are fine, but the same outfit looks considered rather than casual the moment you switch to something cleaner. Pleated trousers or plain cargos both work under a leather jacket, and both handle a full day out better than denim in the heat.

Dressing for the actual weather

  • October and March: jacket open over a tee. It is not cold enough for more.
  • November and February: jacket zipped over a shirt, or open over a hoodie.
  • December and January: hoodie underneath, jacket one size up so it closes comfortably.

Buy less, wear it more

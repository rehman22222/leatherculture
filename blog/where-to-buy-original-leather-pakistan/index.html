Sialkot, Karachi or Lahore: Where to Buy Original Leather in Pakistan

In short: Sialkot is not a grade and Karachi is not a price. We read 99 leather jacket ads and looked up 51 certified leather sites to show what each city can and cannot tell you, where to shop, and how to prove a jacket is original leather before you pay.

Ask around for a real leather jacket in Pakistan and you will get a place, not an answer. "Sialkot se mangwao." "Saddar chale jao." "Panorama try karo." Each tip tells you where to go. None tells you what the jacket is made of, who tanned it, or what happens if it cracks after one winter.

So we counted. On 2 November 2026 we read 99 leather jacket and coat ads on OLX Pakistan, looked up every Pakistani site in the Leather Working Group's public directory, and sorted the member addresses of the country's leather garment exporters' association. We started from the city questions people here actually type into Google, from "which city is famous for leather" to "leather jacket price in Sialkot". This guide answers them, and shows how to prove original leather in any city.

Quick answer

No single city is Pakistan's leather city, because each does a different job. Sialkot has the most garment makers. Karachi has the most sites certified by the Leather Working Group, plus Zainab Market for shoppers. Kasur is a tanning town, not a shopping stop. Lahore has makers, tanneries and the Mall Road shops. A city name can suggest where a jacket was stitched. It cannot tell you the pure leather type, the grade or a fair price, so ask for those wherever you buy.

What did 99 leather jacket ads actually say?

Most city searches end on a classified ad or a social page, so we began there. We searched OLX Pakistan nationally and in five cities using neutral phrases such as "leather jacket" and "leather coat", never "pure" or "original", so the search itself could not inflate the claims. That returned 262 ads. We kept the 99 adult jackets and coats with "leather" in the title and read each full description. Fifteen openly sold faux leather, PU or rexine, which left 84 presented as leather.

The claim arrives first, and the evidence often never does. Of the 41 ads claiming pure, original, genuine or real leather, 22 named neither an animal nor a grade. That leaves you nothing to check.

Some answers are not answers. Twenty-two ads named an animal, but nine of them listed several, along the lines of "we deal in cow, sheep and buffalo", without saying which one made the jacket on offer. Only 13 named one animal.

"Sialkot" was used as proof. Seven ads mentioned Sialkot, usually as "made in Sialkot". None of the seven named an animal or a grade. The city was doing the job the description should have done.

Promises came without terms. Ten ads offered a warranty or guarantee, and none said how long it lasts. Not one of the 84 explained what happens if the jacket has to go back.

A classified ad is not the whole market, and a shopkeeper may know far more than an ad says. Some sellers also posted the same text more than once. Counting each text once leaves 72 ads, with 34 claims, 16 naming an animal, one naming a grade, and still no tannery or return terms, so the pattern holds. The full method is near the end of this guide.

Which city is famous for leather in Pakistan?

It depends which stage of the work you mean, and two public lists show why. The first is the member directory of the Pakistan Leather Garments Manufacturers & Exporters Association, which keeps its two offices in Sialkot and in Korangi, Karachi. The second is the Leather Working Group (LWG) directory of audited leather sites.

Garment makers cluster in Sialkot, and audited tanneries cluster in Karachi. That fits a 2021 industry mapping by the Dutch research group SOMO, which placed apparel factories mainly in Karachi and Sialkot, and footwear in and around Sialkot and Lahore, with another hub in greater Karachi. The lists are only part of the picture: the industry's own estimate of about 800 tanneries, repeated by the rating agency PACRA in April 2026, is far larger than the 51 sites LWG lists.

One more figure explains why shopping here feels so confusing. The Pakistan Tanners Association has described the industry as about 95 per cent export-oriented. In the 11 months to May 2026, provisional Pakistan Bureau of Statistics figures show about $241 million of leather apparel and $281 million of leather gloves exported. These cities earned their fame on work made to foreign buyers' specifications. A jacket sold at home with the same city's name attached did not necessarily go through that process.

A city names one stop on a jacket's journey

Every jacket passes through five stops, and each one decides something different. When a seller says "Sialkot", ask which stop they mean.

  1. Hide. Cow, buffalo, sheep or goat, mostly from animals raised for meat. This sets the leather's natural character, which our guide to pure leather types compares.
  2. Tannery. This turns hide into leather and sets the layer and finish. Trade reporting places most tanneries in clusters in Kasur and Karachi, with another large group in Sialkot.
  3. Stitching. Cutting, lining and hardware happen here. A Dawn feature on Sialkot described makers securing foreign orders first and then subcontracting the work, so "the factory" can be a network of smaller units.
  4. Market. Traders move stock between cities. Profit described one Zainab Market leather outlet that makes its own leatherwear, wholesales through outlets in major cities and also exports.
  5. Listing. Someone writes "pure leather, made in Sialkot". This is the only stop most buyers ever see.

These stops can belong to one firm, since many Pakistani tanners also own garment, shoe and leather goods factories. They can also be spread out. Nothing stops a jacket from being tanned in one city, stitched in a second and sold in a third, which is why a single place name cannot describe it.

Sialkot leather: what the name does and does not promise

Sialkot's reputation is real. Its leather work is about a century old. It began with footballs, added gloves in the 1970s and garments after that. A Dawn profile counted leather garments and gloves among the three industries that dominate the city, and more than 130 of the garment association's members give a Sialkot-area address today, far more than anywhere else.

That reputation belongs to a cluster, not to any one jacket. Makers there work to each buyer's specification and budget, so one town can turn out very different qualities. So "is Sialkot leather good?" has no single answer. We found no official standard behind the phrase "Sialkot leather", and it says nothing about the pure leather type in your hands.

Sialkot leather jacket price: is it really cheaper?

Not in our sample. The 12 priced ads listed in Sialkot asked Rs 4,050 to Rs 30,000, with a median of Rs 15,000. That was above the medians for Lahore and Rawalpindi. Twelve ads prove little, but they are enough to question the idea that a Sialkot address always means a lower price. A very low price with only "made in Sialkot" behind it deserves more questions, not fewer, and an original leather jacket from Sialkot needs the same proof as one from anywhere else.

Can you buy directly from a Sialkot factory?

Often, yes, but find out what kind of business you are talking to. Export factories run on bulk orders, while many single-piece sellers are workshops, traders or pages using the word "factory". Eight ads in our sample used factory, manufacturer or wholesale. None of that is wrong if the terms are clear. Ask for the business name and address, the minimum order, a video of the actual piece, and who pays the courier if it does not fit. You can also see whether an exporter appears in the association's Northern Zone member directory. Membership shows that a firm is known to the trade. It does not grade a jacket.

What about the Sialkot Tannery Zone?

You may see it mentioned. The zone is meant to move the city's scattered tanneries, reported at more than 280 in 2023, onto one site with shared waste treatment. Trade reports in June 2026 said its effluent plant had been inaugurated. A month earlier, tannery owners told The Nation that operations had not started. We found no later report, so do not accept "tanned in the zone" without a tannery's name.

Leather market in Karachi: Korangi makes it, Saddar sells it

Karachi plays two separate roles. Korangi Industrial Area, especially Sector 7-A, is where the work happens. A 2011 Dawn report counted 250 to 260 units in that one sector, and 25 of the 51 LWG sites in Pakistan are in Karachi. It is an industrial estate for trade buyers rather than somewhere to browse.

For shoppers, the name that keeps coming up is Zainab Market in Saddar. In 2009 Dawn described its entire first floor as leather shops selling coats, jackets, belts, shoes and bags. Prices were unmarked, which meant "hard bargaining". The same report described the stock as a mix of export surplus from local factories and "very good copies". A 2017 Profit report said surplus reaches the market because factories produce more than an order needs, while one shopper called the clothing "export-reject quality, with minor flaws". Those reports are old and we did not visit for this guide, so treat them as background and check the current shops yourself.

  • Check the size first. Profit reported oversized leftovers as a real problem, because export sizing does not fit every local buyer. Try the jacket over your winter layer.
  • Do not lead with a brand. The same shopper said the price rises the moment you name one. A label in a bazaar also proves very little.
  • Ask what kind of stock it is. New local, surplus, reject and copy should not cost the same. If there is a flaw, ask to see it before you agree a price.

Only seven priced ads came from Karachi, too few to quote a typical figure. The best answer to "leather jacket price in Karachi" is three quotes on the same day for jackets of the same type.

Leather market in Lahore: Mall Road, Anarkali and the Kasur question

Lahore shoppers mostly ask about Panorama Centre on Mall Road. "Leather jackets in Panorama Lahore" appears in Google's suggestions, yet we could not find an official shop directory. A 2015 city guide describes two basement floors of men's clothing there. Shopper reviews from 2021 to 2023 mention bargains on leatherwear and warn that you have to haggle hard. The same guide lists leather wear among the crafts of Anarkali Bazaar. That is thin evidence, so treat these as places to go and inspect, not places we vouch for.

Lahore was the best-sampled city, with 31 priced ads. They ran from Rs 2,000 to Rs 27,000, and the middle half sat between Rs 6,500 and Rs 15,750. That spread is the useful finding: inside one city, an asking price alone tells you very little.

Should you go to Kasur?

Probably not for one jacket. Kasur matters at the tannery stop. A 2017 Dawn report counted 272 registered tanneries there, in an area intermixed with housing, and four LWG sites are listed today. PACRA takes its local raw material prices from the Lahore and Kasur markets, which points to a trade in hides and materials. Only one jacket ad in our sample was listed in Kasur. If you are buying leather for a workshop, it is worth the trip. If you want a finished jacket, we found no evidence of a retail market there.

Rawalpindi, Islamabad and the Landa bazaar route

In Rawalpindi the best documented route is second-hand. A 2019 Dawn report described the city's old markets, including Coat Wali Gali in Narankari Bazaar, Raja Bazaar, Saddar near the Railways bridge and Jamia Masjid Road, with traders buying in bulk from distributors in Karachi. These bazaars are mainstream: a Gallup Pakistan write-up in 2026 said 48% of Pakistanis report buying used clothing from them.

Are Landa leather jackets original? Many may well be real leather, but the person at the stall can rarely tell you a jacket's history. Imported stock is sorted by category and condition at the wholesale stage, and the price follows from that. So inspect it the way you would a used bike:

  • Collar and cuffs: look for cracks and for edges that have gone hard or shiny.
  • Underarms and lining: check for sweat damage, torn seams and any lining that has been replaced.
  • Surface: a film peeling away in sheets usually points to a coating over fabric or bonded material, not worn hide.
  • Zips and snaps: work each one several times, and price in a repair if one sticks.
  • Smell: a musty smell can mean mould, which matters more in humid cities. Our care guide explains why.

The 11 priced used ads in our sample had a median of Rs 7,000, against Rs 11,000 for the 71 priced as new. That gap is what you are trading for an unknown history and, usually, no way to bring it back.

LWG certified tanneries in Pakistan: check a claim in one minute

People search for this list, so here is what the directory showed on 2 November 2026: 51 Pakistani sites out of 2,170 worldwide. By site address that was 25 in Karachi, 11 in the Lahore area, 8 in Sialkot, 4 in Kasur, 1 in Multan and 2 with no major city in the address. Thirty-nine were rated Gold, 8 Silver and 2 Audited, and 2 showed no rating. Thirty-four list apparel among the industries they supply.

Know what the certificate covers before you are impressed by it. LWG says plainly that "it is the facility that made the leather that carries the certification, not the product", and that its audit does not include a full product assessment. So a logo does not mean a given jacket is soft, well stitched or even made from that site's leather.

  1. Ask for the tannery's name or its LWG reference number, which the directory calls a URN.
  2. Search it in LWG's certified supplier directory, which is free and public.
  3. Read the audit expiry date. Three of the 51 entries showed a date that had already passed on the day we looked.
  4. Compare the details. Each entry lists animal types and leather types. If a seller says cowhide and the site lists only sheep and goat, ask why.
  5. Ask how this jacket links to that site. A batch record or invoice is an answer. A logo on a page is not.

The ten-minute market test

Use this to check for original leather at any shop, in any city. It needs only your phone and a little patience, and you should do it before you bargain.

  1. Ask two questions before you touch anything. "Which animal is this?" and "Is this full grain, corrected grain or a split?" Someone who knows will answer straight away. If the only reply is "100% pure", you have learned something too.
  2. Find a raw edge. Use your phone torch inside the hem, the lining zip or a pocket. Fibres suggest hide, while a woven fabric backing suggests a coated material. Our real versus faux guide shows what to look for.
  3. Compare two panels. Hide usually varies a little between the chest and the sleeve. A grain that repeats exactly points to an embossed surface, which can be corrected leather or a synthetic.
  4. Fold a sleeve and let go. Look for fine creases that relax. Cracking, whitening or a plastic look at the fold are warnings.
  5. Put it on over your winter layer. Raise both arms, and sit down if you drive or ride. Measure the chest and sleeve against our size guide, because surplus labels may not match local sizes.
  6. Work the hardware. Run each zip several times, press each snap, and look along the armhole and collar seams for loose stitches.
  7. Ask what kind of stock it is. New, surplus, reject or used. If there is a flaw, get it pointed out now.
  8. Skip the lighter and the water. Burning or wetting a jacket you have not bought is unfair and proves less than people think. A cut edge and honest answers tell you more.
  9. Bargain last. Prices may not be marked, so get a second quote for the same type from another shop first.
  10. Get it in writing. Ask for the material and any exchange terms on the bill, or photograph a handwritten note. Without that you have very little to fall back on.

Buying from another city? Send this first

If you cannot visit, make the seller do the showing. Copy this into WhatsApp or Instagram. It asks for the proof of original leather that 84 ads left out.

Salam. I need a few details about this jacket before I order: (1) the animal and the layer, for example full-grain cow; (2) where the leather was tanned, and the tannery's LWG number if you have one; (3) where it was stitched, and your business name and address; (4) whether it is new, export surplus, reject or used; (5) its chest, shoulder, sleeve and length in inches; (6) a short video of this exact piece showing an inside seam and a raw edge; (7) if the size or material is not as described, how many days I have, how I send it back and who pays the courier.

Salam. Jacket mangwane se pehle mujhe yeh tafseel chahiye: (1) leather ka janwar aur layer, jaise full-grain gaaye; (2) leather kahan tan hua, aur tannery ka LWG number agar ho; (3) silai kahan hui, aur aap ke business ka naam aur pata; (4) jacket nayi hai, export surplus, reject ya used; (5) chest, shoulder, sleeve aur length inches mein; (6) isi jacket ki short video jis mein andar ki silai aur kata hua kinara nazar aaye; (7) agar size ya material theek na nikla to kitne din mein, kaise wapas hogi aur courier kaun dega.

  • A strong reply names the hide and layer, shows your actual jacket, gives a business name and states the terms.
  • A weak reply says "100% pure sir", "Sialkot ka maal hai" or "export quality", and sends a catalogue photo.

Leather jacket kahan se khareedein?

Agar aap ne Google par "leather jacket kahan se khareedein" ya "Sialkot ki leather jacket" likha hai, to seedha jawab yeh hai: Pakistan mein koi aik leather ka shehar nahi. Sialkot mein jackets aur gloves banane wale sab se zyada hain. Karachi aur Kasur mein tanneries hain. Dukaanen aur bazaar Karachi ke Saddar, Lahore ke Mall Road aur Anarkali, aur Pindi ke Landa bazaaron mein milte hain.

Shehar ka naam sirf itna batata hai ke jacket shayad kahan silai hui. Yeh nahi batata ke leather kis janwar ka hai, kaunsi layer hai, ya qeemat theek hai ya nahi. Is liye dukaandar ya online seller se yeh paanch sawal zaroor poochein:

  1. Leather kis janwar ka hai: gaaye, bhains, bakri ya bhair?
  2. Full grain hai, corrected hai ya split?
  3. Leather kahan tan hua, aur jacket kahan silai hui?
  4. Maal naya hai, export surplus hai, reject hai ya Landa ka?
  5. Agar size ya maal theek na nikla to wapsi kaise hogi, aur courier kaun dega?

Jo seller seedha jawab de, us par bharosa karna aasan hai. Jo sirf "100% pure hai, Sialkot ka maal hai" kahe, us se pehle saboot maangein, phir paise dein.

How we did this, and what it cannot tell you

  • Searches. We used Google's search suggestions for Pakistan on 2 November 2026. Suggestions show that a phrase is searched, not how often.
  • Ads. We ran five neutral phrases on OLX Pakistan, nationally, in Punjab and Sindh, and in Sialkot, Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. We counted each ad under the city printed on it.
  • Counting words. A claim word counted only when it described the leather or the jacket, so "original zips" did not. "Not rexine" was not counted as synthetic. One ad had no description, so only its title was read. We counted what sellers wrote, and we did not test any jacket.
  • Prices. We removed one Rs 100 placeholder, which left 83 priced ads. Cities with fewer than 10 priced ads get no typical figure.
  • Directories. We sorted association members and LWG entries by the address shown. Neither list covers every business, and either can change.
  • Limits. OLX is one marketplace on one day, and repeat posts exist. We did not visit the markets, and some market reports are years old, so we give their dates. We name no seller, and nothing here is a recommendation of any shop.

Frequently asked questions about buying leather by city in Pakistan

Where can I buy original leather in Pakistan?

You can find original leather through Sialkot makers, city markets such as Zainab Market in Karachi, shops on Mall Road in Lahore, Landa bazaars and online stores. The place matters less than the proof, so buy where the seller will name the animal and layer, show the actual piece and put the terms in writing.

Which city is famous for leather in Pakistan?

No single city holds that title. Sialkot has the most leather garment makers, with 134 association members by our count. Karachi has the most LWG certified leather sites, at 25 of 51, plus Zainab Market. Kasur is a long-established tanning town, and Lahore combines makers, tanneries and shops.

Is Sialkot leather good?

It can be excellent or poor, because Sialkot makers work to each buyer's specification and budget. The name is not a grade, and we found no official standard behind it. Judge the jacket itself: its animal, layer, finish, stitching and the seller's terms.

Are leather jackets made in Pakistan good?

Pakistan has a serious export industry, with about $241 million of leather apparel shipped in the 11 months to May 2026 on provisional figures. That shows capability, not the quality of a particular jacket. Check each piece as you would a jacket from anywhere else.

What is the leather jacket price in Sialkot?

In our small sample of 12 OLX ads on 2 November 2026, asks ran from Rs 4,050 to Rs 30,000, with a median of Rs 15,000. These are asking prices for jackets of unknown hide and grade, so compare like with like before deciding.

Where is the leather market in Karachi?

For shoppers, the name to know is Zainab Market in Saddar, where past reports place leather shops on the first floor. Korangi Industrial Area is where tanning and manufacturing happen, and it mainly serves trade buyers. Check current shops and hours before you travel.

Where can I buy a leather jacket in Lahore?

Shoppers most often look at Panorama Centre on Mall Road and at Anarkali Bazaar. We could not find an official directory of leather shops in either, so go prepared to inspect, compare and bargain.

What is the difference between export quality, export surplus and export reject?

"Export quality" is a sales phrase with no fixed meaning that we could find. "Export surplus" means extra stock left over from an export order. "Export reject" means it missed a buyer's inspection, sometimes for a small flaw, so ask to be shown the flaw.

Are Landa leather jackets original?

Many may be real leather, but the seller can rarely prove a jacket's history, and a label can mislead. Inspect the collar, cuffs, underarms, lining, zips and surface. Used jackets in our sample asked a median of Rs 7,000, usually with no way to return them.

Which pure leather type should I ask for?

No type wins everywhere. Reach for cow or buffalo if the jacket will take rough daily wear, goat if you want something light, and sheep if softness matters most and you will treat it gently. Our pure leather types guide compares them, along with layers and finishes.

How many LWG certified tanneries are there in Pakistan?

LWG's public directory listed 51 Pakistani sites on 2 November 2026, of which 41 were leather manufacturers and 10 commissioning manufacturers. Karachi had the most. The list changes, so check the directory and each site's audit expiry date yourself.

Where are most tanneries in Pakistan?

Trade reporting places most of them in clusters in Kasur and Karachi, especially Korangi, with another large group in Sialkot. The industry's own estimate is about 800 tanneries nationally, though published counts vary and many are old.

How do I know a jacket is original leather before I pay?

Find a raw edge with your phone torch, compare two panels for a repeating grain, fold a sleeve to see how it creases, and ask the seller to name the animal and layer. No single check is proof, but together they catch most problems.

Does original leather peel?

Real leather can scuff, dry out or crack when it is neglected. A plastic-like film peeling away in sheets usually points to a coating over fabric or bonded material. Heavily pigmented leather can also lose its surface coat, so check a raw edge if you are unsure.

Buy the answer, not the address

Sialkot, Karachi and Lahore have all earned their place in Pakistan's leather story. None of them can vouch for the jacket in front of you. Take the questions with you, wherever you shop, and pay for original leather only when the seller can answer them.

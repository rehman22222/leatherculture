# LeatherCulture SEO audit and implementation report

**Date:** 19 October 2026 (Asia/Karachi)  
**Website:** https://www.leatherculture.shop  
**Market:** Pakistan; English content  
**Scope:** all 16 existing sitemap pages, the new article, generated assets, responsive footer, catalogue behaviour, metadata, internal linking, structured data, crawl controls, keyword research and available third-party performance/authority data.

## Result

The site had working canonicals, sitemap routes and structured data, but several content and presentation problems remained. These have been corrected in the build/runtime code and in the published content. The final automated checks cover **17 public pages**. This is a technical and content audit with Ubersuggest research, not a verified Search Console indexing or traffic audit.

## Findings and implemented fixes

| Priority | Finding | Action |
|---|---|---|
| High | The footer replaced a complete phone icon/text component with a narrow, unstyled email fallback. The main email could clip. | Preserve nested markup; hide the redundant phone row when no public phone exists; add responsive contact columns, readable email text and complete inline SVG icons. |
| High | New admin page titles and descriptions were not materialized into the static HTML. | Apply current page metadata during the build to title, description, social tags and relevant page schema. Added regression checks. |
| High | Some journal cards linked to real articles while displaying template titles/dates or unrelated excerpts. | Render current article names, categories, excerpts, dates, reading times and optimized images into initial HTML. Added destination/title checks. |
| High | Runtime catalogue mapping could use up the product list in one section and leave another section without its own cards after hydration. | Assign live products separately within each section. Verified all five Best Sellers cards after interaction, with the backend request blocked. |
| Medium | The shop description advertised coats and shirts despite the current five-jacket catalogue. Several page titles used vague superlatives. | Updated the five main pages' SEO metadata to describe their actual purpose, include LeatherCulture and match relevant search intent. The prior metadata is saved in `seo-metadata-before.json`. |
| Medium | The fixed number of template journal slots could leave future articles poorly linked. | Added an all-guides list on the journal page. New article is linked from the homepage/journal and related articles; it links to all five products and the four existing guides. |
| Medium | Brand CSS was copied to the build, but pages retained older inline copies. Long-lived script caching could also preserve old code. | Generate a content-hashed brand stylesheet and content-version the override script. |
| Medium | No article answered the price-comparison buying questions identified in keyword research. | Published an original buying guide with a dated catalogue price table, material/fit questions, delivery terms, FAQs and relevant links. |
| Low | Blog tables and editorial images needed reliable mobile presentation. | Added responsive table scrolling, explicit image dimensions, a lazy-loaded WebP editorial image and readable article styles. |

## Published article

**Title:** Leather jacket price in Pakistan: what to check before you buy  
**URL:** https://www.leatherculture.shop/blog/leather-jacket-price-pakistan  
**Body length:** approximately 1,350 words, including table/TOC/FAQ text.  
**Author:** LeatherCulture team; published 19 October 2026.

The article answers the buying question directly, uses the store's actual Rs 15,000 catalogue prices, distinguishes dated information from current checkout information, and avoids inventing animal species, leather grades or return promises. It includes a single H1, descriptive H2/H3 sections, a table of contents, a comparison table, FAQs, canonical URL, description, Open Graph/Twitter tags, BlogPosting/BreadcrumbList/Organization schema and sitemap inclusion.

The copy was written for this site rather than copied or paraphrased from a competitor. **No commercial plagiarism scan was available, so a similarity score of 15% or less is not certified.** Keyword placement is natural; adding a keywords tag alone is not treated as a ranking strategy.

Images: the cover uses the store's existing Heritage jacket WebP. The body uses a licensed photograph by El gringo photo, downloaded from Pexels, converted to a **128,602-byte WebP**, and credited in a visible caption. It is explicitly identified as an editorial example, not a LeatherCulture product or endorsement. See [image credits](blog-image-credits.md) and the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

## Remaining requirements for measurable ranking work

1. **Search Console access:** the owner subsequently provided screenshots confirming the site's domain property. The connected tools still do not expose its queries, impressions, CTR or URL Inspection data. Confirm the existing sitemap in that property; no submission or indexing claim is made here. See the [Search Console follow-up](SEARCH-CONSOLE-FIXES-2026-10-19.md).
2. **Material facts:** all five product records have an empty material field. Confirm shell material, lining, measurements and care instructions with the owner/supplier before adding those facts. They must not be invented for SEO.
3. **Returns policy — resolved in the follow-up:** the owner confirmed refund requests within 7 days of receipt for undamaged products, with return courier paid by the customer. The policy and merchant schema now use those terms. The confirmed contact address is `contact@leatherculture.shop`.
4. **Business profiles:** existing social URLs are service homepages, so the site correctly hides them. Supply the brand's real profile URLs before adding them to links or Organization schema.
5. **Authority and customer evidence:** obtain legitimate brand mentions and genuine customer reviews. Do not buy spam links or create fabricated reviews to raise a metric.
6. **Measurement limits:** At research time, Ubersuggest was authenticated on a free account. Its paid site-crawl report was not run and no paid credits were used. SEOmatic/Search Console, AI-answer visibility and a commercial plagiarism checker were unavailable. These are explicit gaps rather than completed checks. A paid Ubersuggest plan would enable its hosted crawl, but the code/public-page audit above is already completed.

Implementation follows the relevant [Google ecommerce site-structure guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/help-google-understand-your-ecommerce-site-structure) and [product structured-data guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product). The report does not promise rankings or rich-result eligibility. The remaining work depends on verified business facts and actual Search Console measurements.
